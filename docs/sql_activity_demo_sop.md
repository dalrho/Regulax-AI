# Standard Operating Procedure: Controlled Document Review

**Document ID:** SOP-QA-001  
**Version:** 1  
**Owner:** Quality Assurance  
**Status:** Demonstration copy

## 1. Purpose

Describe a repeatable process for receiving, reviewing, approving, revising, and
retaining controlled procedures in the Regulax workspace. This sample is for
demonstration and training; adapt it to the organization's approved quality
system before operational use.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to internal standard operating procedures and work
instructions stored in the workspace SOP Library. It covers document owners,
reviewers, and workspace owners who manage access to controlled documents.

## 3. Responsibilities

- **Document owner:** prepares the procedure, checks that it is current, and
  submits revisions for review.
- **Reviewer:** checks the draft for accuracy, clarity, completeness, and
  consistency with approved internal requirements; records comments for the
  owner.
- **Workspace owner:** manages workspace membership and ensures that access is
  assigned to the appropriate people.

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 Create or receive a document

1. Confirm the document has a clear title, purpose, scope, owner, and document
   identifier.
2. Check that the content is readable and that referenced forms or records can
   be identified.
3. Upload the approved text to the correct workspace SOP Library entry. Do not
   place confidential personal or customer information in a demonstration
   document.

### 4.2 Review the document

1. The owner submits the draft to an assigned reviewer.
2. The reviewer checks responsibilities, process steps, decision points,
   required records, and references.
3. The reviewer returns comments to the owner. The owner resolves each comment
   or records why a suggested change was not accepted.
4. The owner submits the revised text for a final review when material changes
   were made.

### 4.3 Approve and publish

1. Confirm that the title, document identifier, owner, and version label are
   correct.
2. Record the approval decision and effective date in the organization's
   approved recordkeeping system.
3. Make the approved version available to the people who need it and remove
   obsolete working copies from routine use.

### 4.4 Revise and retain versions

1. Start a new version when a process, responsibility, form, or reference
   changes.
2. Preserve the previously stored version as a historical record. Do not
   overwrite an approved version's contents.
3. Review the version history when confirming which procedure was current at a
   particular time.

### 4.5 Periodic review

The document owner reviews the procedure at the interval established by the
organization, and sooner when a process, product, applicable requirement, or
identified risk changes. Record the review outcome and any follow-up action.

## 5. Records

Retain the controlled document, its version history, review comments, approval
evidence, and review outcome according to the organization's records-retention
procedure.
